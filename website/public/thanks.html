Welcome to StreamSafe

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Your free trial has started. The email from Dodo Payments has your licence key and the download link for the app (check spam too).

1. Download the app

Open the email and press the download button for StreamSafe for Windows. The same download is on the purchase page that opened after checkout.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

2. Activate

Open StreamSafe, go to Settings, then Subscription, paste your licence key and press Activate.

3. Connect and go live

Pick your platform, paste your stream key, put the address StreamSafe shows into OBS, press Start, then Start Streaming in OBS.

Need help? Email streamsafe1@gmail.com.
